Who We Are

Our website address is mlmservices.vkservicesenterprise.com. We provide professional services related to MLM software solutions and MLM plan promotions.

02

Comments

When visitors leave comments on our site, we collect the data shown in the comments form, along with the visitor's IP address and browser user agent to help detect spam.

An anonymized string (hash) from your email address may be sent to the Gravatar service to verify if you use it. Once approved, your profile picture may be publicly visible next to your comment.

03

Media

If you upload images, please ensure they do not contain EXIF GPS location data. Visitors may be able to download and extract location data from such images.

04

Cookies

  • –If you leave a comment, you can opt-in to save your name, email, and website in cookies for convenience. These cookies last 1 year.
  • –Visiting the login page sets a temporary cookie to check browser compatibility. It's deleted when the browser is closed.
  • –Logging in sets cookies to save your login info and display preferences. Login cookies last 2 days, and display cookies last 1 year. "Remember Me" extends login to 2 weeks.
  • –Editing or publishing a post saves a cookie that expires after 1 day.
05

Embedded Content

Articles on our site may include embedded content (like videos, images, articles). Embedded content from other websites behaves exactly as if you visited those websites directly.

These websites may collect data, use cookies, and monitor your interaction with their content, especially if you're logged in to their platform.

06

Who We Share Your Data With
